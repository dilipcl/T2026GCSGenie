GCSE Genie - Master Software Specification & Prompt Blueprint (v3)
This master specification acts as a direct software blueprint. It is designed to be pasted directly into LLM terminals (e.g., Gemini Advanced, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, or GPT-4o) to generate a complete, working application.

It is tailored for a student (Tejas Dilip, GCS Year 10) preparing for GCSE exams, incorporating direct diagnostic feedback from his real-world Year 9 academic assessments and Guildford County School (GCS) guidelines. It should be accessible via mobile and laptop.

1. System Philosophy & Core Constraints
100% Manual, Private, & Offline: Zero integrations with external school portals (Sparx, Bromcom, MCAS, Teams). Everything is manually entered by the student or parents. Teachers have no access.
[SUPERSEDED] "Offline" and "nothing leaves the device" no longer hold in full. Optional Dexie Cloud
sync was added in August 2026 so parent oversight works across devices; it is off until a user signs
in. Zero school-portal integration and zero teacher access remain true. The LLM API key is excluded
from sync and from backups.
Zero-Administrative Overhead: The student's dashboard is optimized for rapid, frictionless interactions. Daily check-ins must take under 2 minutes using quick-toggle buttons, sliders, and tap-to-complete check-ins.
Multi-User Collaborative Access:
Student Role: Complete daily tasks, log homework, check off revision blocks, view current XP, and request real-world rewards.
Parent Role: Monitor consistency, manage the rewards ledger (approve/deny points redemption), enter schedule overrides, manually log school sanctions (detentions), and trigger the "Agent Audit" service.
Basic Audit Service: To preserve data trust and prevent tampering, every insert, update, or deletion of a goal, task, score, or points deduction must write to a write-only audit trail logging: [Timestamp, User, Field, Action, Old Value, New Value].

The app tracks progress across the student's core and elective GCSE subjects, aligned to exact exam boards:

3. Real-World Assessment Reference & "Remediation Actions"
Instead of generic study targets, the app has a dedicated portal to refer to real-world test papers. It translates specific errors from his actual Year 9 exam scripts and GCS interim reports (IR3) into high-value Remediation Actions:

A. Mathematics (Source: yr9- maths.pdf - Score: 60/75, Sec 3: 16/25)
Action 1: Venn Diagram Probability Proofs (+200 XP)
Diagnostic Error: Scored 0/2 on proving event independence.
Task: Solve 3 mock exam questions proving independence using the formula: $P(B \cap S) = P(B) \times P(S)$.
Action 2: Scale-Factor Enlargements with Coordinate Centers (+150 XP)
Diagnostic Error: Scored 1/2 on scale-factor enlargement and transformation descriptions.
Task: Complete 2 graph-based exercises performing shape enlargement with negative fractional scale factors and specified $(X,Y)$ centers of origin.
Action 3: Double Bracket Algebra Simplification (+100 XP)
Diagnostic Error: Calculation errors on expanding $(2x+3)^2 - (2x+3)(x-5)$.
Task: Solve 5 complex expanding and factorizing quadratic expressions to reinforce sign consistency.
B. Science (Source: science .pdf - Year 9 End of Year Assessment)
Action 1: Chromatography Rf Value Calculations (+150 XP)
Diagnostic Error: Erroneously calculated an Rf value of 5.6 by reversing the numerator and denominator.
Task: Solve 5 chromatogram calculation problems. Ensure the correct formula is used ($\text{Rf} = \frac{\text{Distance moved by solute}}{\text{Distance moved by solvent}}$) and verify that the output value is strictly less than 1.0.
Action 2: Physics Power-to-Energy Unit Conversions (+200 XP)
Diagnostic Error: Multiplied $65\text{W}$ by $25\text{ mins}$ directly on a drone battery question, omitting the conversion of minutes to seconds, resulting in $1625\text{ J}$ instead of $97,500\text{ J}$.
Task: Solve 5 energy transfer problems involving kinetic energy, gravitational potential, and power. Explicitly include a "Unit Conversion Safety Check" step before executing final calculations.
C. History (Source: history.pdf - Score: 20.5/28)
Action 1: Reparations & Germany Keyword Mastery (+100 XP)
Diagnostic Error: Lost marks on the precise definition of Weimar Germany "Reparations".
Task: Match key terms (Reparations, Diktat, Demilitarisation, Lebensraum, Propaganda) to correct definitions. (Reparations = war damage compensation, specifically set at £6.6 billion in the Treaty of Versailles).
Action 2: Essay Structure "Comparison Quest" (+250 XP)
Diagnostic Error: Lost 4 marks on the 12-mark Treaty of Versailles essay for failing to explicitly compare land losses vs. reparations as the primary source of national humiliation.
Task: Outline a standard 3-paragraph GCS essay skeleton that directly compares two historical causes and concludes with a definitive, justified judgment.
D. Computer Science (Source: ir report.pdf - GCS Interim Report IR3)
Action 1: CS Homework Consistency Challenge (+300 XP)
Diagnostic Error: IR3 highlighted that while class commitment was good, Computer Science Home Learning under teacher AMN was "Below the expected standard" due to missing assignments.
Task: Maintain a 14-day streak of logging and completing Computer Science tasks on the exact day they are set.

5. SMART Goal Consultation & Burnout Prevention Modules
A. Student-Parent Goal Consultation Flow
To ensure goals are realistic and collaborative, a dedicated module allows the student to propose academic, co-curricular, or personal goals:

Drafting: The student drafts a goal (e.g., "Join a local football club").
SMART Criteria Form: The app prompts them to define:
Specific: Which club and what position?
Measurable: How many training sessions per week?
Achievable: Does the training fit into his schedule?
Realistic: Can this be done without hurting academic performance?
Time-bound: By what month will trials be completed?
Parental Review & Locking: The goal enters a "Pending Discussion" state. During weekly reviews, parents can approve, adjust, and lock the goal. Once locked, it generates daily/weekly habits that are added to his calendar.

GCSE grade 9 must be goal and there need to be specific tasks and criterias based on it which can be mapped to all the tasks.
There need to be visualisation in the dashboard that provides a simple view of the goals with RAG status and feasibility to drill down further if required. Also the app should appear as a daily organiser where the student can upload the time table, there is a calendar where it is easy to add some reminders. There also need to be a general help section which has details on the subject teachers and contact details and provision to add notes, websites with free content, mock tests, career learning content to get an insight for Tejas. It also need to be in the tasks so that he gets to understand more on the career path and importance of grades for post GCSE phase.
B. Time-Budget Matrix & Burnout Risk Heatmap
To prevent academic and physical exhaustion, the system maintains a Time-Capacity Budget:

Safe Weekly Focused Activity Threshold: 45 hours.  [SUPERSEDED -> 60 hours; see README "Time budget"]
Standard Weekly Commitments:
GCS School Hours: 32.5 hours
Air Cadets: 6 hours (Tue/Fri 19:00 - 22:00)
Art support class: 1.5 hours
Drums + practice: 2 hours
DofE physical/volunteering: 2 hours
Total Base Commitment: 44 hours/week (leaving 1 hour of safe capacity).  [SUPERSEDED -> 44h baseline against a 60h ceiling, leaving 16h of study headroom]
Burnout Risk Notification: If the student attempts to add a new personal goal (such as "Join a Football Club" with 4 hours of training and weekend matches), the app calculates the projected total (48 hours), moves the "Stress Index" into the Red Zone (Burnout Risk), and issues a warning to parents and student.
Prioritization (MoSCoW Rules): During mock exams or final Art practical sessions, the app automatically recommends postponing or scaling back secondary, non-essential goals to restore safe time budgets.
6. Points, Penalties, & Real-World Rewards
To make gamification meaningful, points translate directly into parent-managed, real-world incentives:

XP Earning Rates:
Daily 2-minute check-in: +10 XP (first check-in of the day only)
Completed GCS Homework: +50 XP (+60 if High priority). Credited once, via the task itself.
Completed Year 9 Remediation Action: +150 to +300 XP
School Sanctions & Detentions:
Since GCS has a strict detention policy, detentions must be logged manually.
Action: Logging a detention applies a -500 XP penalty and freezes the Rewards Shop.
Remediation Quest: He must complete a parent-assigned academic task (e.g., "Log 45 minutes of focused English revision") to lift the freeze and unlock the shop.
Rewards Shop & Contract:
Student requests redemption: e.g., "Redeem 1,000 XP for 1 Hour Extra Screen Time".
Action: The request goes to the Parent Portal. Parents manually approve, deny, or adjust the request during weekly check-ins, reinforcing real-world rewards.
7. Parent-Initiated "Agentic Audit" Process
To ensure the app remains an asset and does not become an administrative chore, the Parent Portal features an Agentic Audit Button:

Initiation: Parent triggers the audit agent.
Analysis: The agent evaluates:
Activity Logs (compliance with the 2-minute daily check-in).
Weekly time budget (assessing if the student is encroaching on sleep/rest).
Subject balance (checking if certain subjects, like Computer Science homework, are being neglected).
Points-to-Rewards ratios (auditing the points economy).
Output Report: The agent compiles a concise "Plan Alignment Report" summarizing:
Curriculum Status: What subjects are on track vs. falling behind.
Burnout Level: Current Stress Index and time allocation.
Actionable Adjustments: Recommending changes to the calendar, task frequencies, or pausing personal goals.

APPENDIX: DIVERGENCES FROM THIS BRIEF (reconciled August 2026)
================================================================================
What the brief asked for vs. what exists. Full detail in
GCSE_Genie_Architecture_Spec.md section 8.

DELIVERED AS SPECIFIED
  - <2 minute daily check-in with quick toggles, sliders and tap-to-complete
  - Year 9 diagnostic remediation quests with mark schemes and XP
  - Odd/Even rotational timetable with hard-locked cadet blocks
  - Subject RAG matrix with drill-down to topic checklists
  - XP economy, rewards shop, parent-logged sanctions with shop freeze
  - PIN-protected Parent Portal, JSON backup/restore, append-only change log
  - Careers hub, teacher directory, curated free revision links

DELIVERED DIFFERENTLY
  - Sync is Dexie Cloud, not encrypted JSON bundles in Google Drive. The JSON
    export remains, but as backup only - it is a whole-database replace and was
    never a safe reconciliation mechanism.
  - Weekly ceiling is 60h, not 45h. At 44h of fixed commitments a 45h ceiling
    left under an hour a week for homework, so the burnout gauge read CRITICAL
    permanently and carried no signal.
  - RAG weighting is 40% homework / 35% remediations / 25% topic mastery. The
    brief's 35% "assessment average" term was dropped because no assessment
    results were stored anywhere in the app. [UPDATED Aug 2026] Assessment
    results now exist (Proof Log) and the per-subject average is calculated and
    displayed, but is still not folded into the score - doing so would move
    every subject's RAG status and is left as an explicit open decision.
  - Navigation is tiered by frequency of use (daily vs weekly) rather than by
    module. Goal creation and amendment is a weekly-tier activity; adding
    homework and key dates is a floating action button on every screen.

BUILT SINCE THIS BRIEF WAS LAST RECONCILED (August 2026)
  - Parental review and LOCKING of proposed goals. Parents now have Approve &
    Lock; unlocked goals display their weekly hours struck through, so the fact
    that they are not counted in the time budget is visible rather than silent.
  - Assessment/mock result tracking, as the Proof Log: per-question marks, the
    cause of each dropped mark, teacher feedback, and photographs of the paper.
    Wrong answers optionally become fix-up tasks due in three days.
  - Cross-device sync, via Dexie Cloud. Optional, off until sign-in, one login
    per device lasting months.

NOT BUILT
  - Enforced parent authority. The PIN gates the interface, not the data: anyone
    with devtools can rewrite IndexedDB and approve their own rewards. Dexie
    Cloud realms and roles would make it real. This is now the largest remaining
    gap in the parent-facing half of this brief.
  - Offline capability and PWA installation (no manifest, no service worker).
    The data layer is offline-first; the assets are not cached.
  - Sleep logging in the check-in (energy and focus only).

KNOWN DEFECTS
  - [FIXED Aug 2026] Live LLM audits never reached Gemini or Claude. The header,
    both default models, the token ceiling and the missing OpenAI path are all
    corrected, and the portal now states why the offline engine ran when it does.
  - [FIXED Aug 2026] Backup export omitted the milestones table, so every restore
    destroyed all key dates and exam milestones. The exporter now walks the live
    schema so no table can be omitted, and restore takes a rescue copy first.
  - The audit ledger is described in this brief as tamper-evident and hash-chained.
    Each entry is hashed independently with no previous-hash link, and rows are
    deletable. Still true; the UI wording was corrected to "Change History".
  - Only Monday of the Odd week is seeded in the timetable. Quick Add can now
    write a lesson to several days at once, which makes filling it in quick.
